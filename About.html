My story

The unconventional path

Why I work differently

I came into data through accounting and data science, not software engineering. That combination is the whole of my practice.

Accounting showed me how businesses actually run — what a chart of accounts is for, what a controller reconciles at month-end. Data science gave me the modelling and the engineering. Most consultants understand the tooling. Fewer understand the accounts underneath it, and that's where reporting projects fail.

What this means for you

You get someone who speaks both languages. I model your chart of accounts correctly, reconcile the result back to your accounting system, and explain it to your board without jargon. That's why my models survive contact with a finance team.

The problem I solve

One site, reporting is arithmetic. Fifteen sites, it's a definitions problem. Each location recorded things slightly differently. Someone builds a roll-up, it doesn't reconcile, and from then on every performance conversation starts with an argument about the number instead of a decision about the site.

I build the layer where a metric is defined once and applied everywhere — conformed dimensions. Financial, operational and benchmarking figures then filter together instead of contradicting each other.

What I've actually built

An end-to-end Microsoft Fabric platform for a multi-property hotel portfolio: Medallion architecture, a Kimball gold layer with conformed dimensions, 600+ DAX measures, deployment pipelines across development, test and production, and tie-out reports validating every figure against the accounting system.

Compliance reporting for a City of Melbourne laneway waste audit program, engaged through Waste Audit & Consultancy Services — moving it from how much non-compliance existed to where it concentrated. Plus the unglamorous work that matters: Xero and QuickBooks integration, month-end acceleration, and spreadsheet systems that behave.

How I actually work

You get me — not a team, not junior consultants, not an engagement partner between you and the work. Based in Canberra, working across Australia remotely and onsite here.

The obvious objection is what happens if I'm unavailable. I develop in the .pbip format with Git version control, so your reporting has change history, diffs, review and rollback. The architecture is documented, not remembered. Your reporting isn't trapped in my file.

Whether I can help

The test: do you operate multiple sites, and do your numbers get argued about?

If so, yes. If you run a single site, or want a dashboard built cheaply from data as it stands, I'm not your best option. Sometimes the answer is that you have a process problem and no amount of Power BI will fix it — and I'd rather say that in week one.
