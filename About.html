My story

The unconventional path

Why I work differently

I came to data through accounting, not software. That's unusual, and it's the whole point.

Accounting taught me how a business actually adds up — what belongs in each account, what a finance manager checks at month-end, and why two reports can both look right and still disagree. The engineering came afterwards. Most people in my field know the software. Fewer know the books underneath it, and that's where reporting goes wrong.

What this means for you

I speak both languages. I build your reporting so it matches your accounts, then prove the figures tie back to your accounting system line by line. And I explain it in plain English to whoever has to sign it off.

The problem I solve

With one site, reporting is just adding up. With fifteen, it becomes an argument.

Every site records things slightly differently. Someone adds them together. The total doesn't match the parts. After that, every meeting starts by debating the number instead of deciding what to do about it.

I fix the cause rather than the symptom: each measure gets defined once, in one place, and applied to every site. Your revenue, your costs and your site rankings then agree with each other, because they all come from the same source.

What I've built

For a hotel group with multiple properties: one reporting platform that pulls every property's data in automatically, giving profit and loss per property, fair comparisons between them, and the reporting their lenders and investors ask for — all from a single model, checked against their accounting system.

For a City of Melbourne street audit programme (through Waste Audit & Consultancy Services): reporting that showed not just how much non-compliance there was, but exactly where it was concentrated.

And plenty of unglamorous work that matters — connecting Xero and QuickBooks to live reporting, cutting month-end down, and fixing spreadsheets that keep breaking.

How I work

You get me. No team, no junior staff, no account manager in between. Based in Canberra — onsite here, and remote for clients across Australia, New Zealand and the US.

The fair question is what happens if I'm not around. So I build things you don't depend on me for: every change to your reporting is tracked and reversible, the design is written down rather than kept in my head, and your team gets trained to run it. Your reporting doesn't live on my laptop.

Can I help you?

One test: do you run more than one site, and do your numbers get argued about?

If so, that's my work. Start with a short review before committing to a build — I'll look at how your data moves from each site to the decisions it feeds, tell you where it breaks, and give you a clear picture of what fixing it involves.
