When the Microsoft partner enrolment is approved:

1. Download the Microsoft Partner badge from Partner Center (Benefits > Logo builder) and save it as logos/microsoft-partner-badge.png
2. In every HTML file, find  hidden data-partner  and delete the word  hidden  (three or four places per file).
3. In every HTML file, replace  PARTNER_PROFILE_URL  with your partner directory profile link.
4. Commit. The badge and "Microsoft Partner" mark appear in the hero, footer and About sidebar.

Wording: "Microsoft Partner" only. Not "Solutions Partner", not "certified partner".
