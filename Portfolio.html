Hotel portfolio: one reporting platform across 50 hotels

Australia · February 2026 to present · Phased delivery, sign-off at each milestone
Hospitality · Microsoft Fabric

What it replaced

Property workbooks that could not feed Power BI at all. Benchmarking across dozens of sheets, debt schedules and budgets across dozens more, investment summaries with duplicate and missing codes. No group-level view, no fair comparison, and a week of assembly every time a lender asked a question.

How

Each property's files land automatically from SharePoint into Fabric, are standardised so every hotel's data means the same thing, and feed one governed model published across dev, test and production. Size-adjusted benchmarking. Tie-out reporting against the ledger. Delivered lean: the first properties were reporting from the new model within weeks, each phase in use and signed off before the next began. In delivery now: brand compliance, row-level security, governance and training.

What they can do now

Compare every hotel on equal terms regardless of size. See profit by property and by revenue line, rooms and dining. Produce the reporting lenders and investors ask for on demand, with every figure proved back to the accounting system. Five audiences report from the same numbers and cannot contradict each other.

City of Melbourne laneway audit: compliance reporting as evidence

Engaged via Waste Audit and Consultancy Services · June 2025 to present · Ongoing support
Local government · Power BI

What it replaced

Audit records and contractor feeds on different daily, weekly and monthly cycles, tracked by hand in Excel. Problems visible weeks after they occurred, with no way to see where they clustered.

How

Internal audit and contractor sources integrated into one governed star-schema model handling three refresh cadences via SharePoint. Governed datasets published in Power BI Service with metadata documentation and ongoing support.

Why it matters beyond waste. Regulators and public programs don't want more numbers; they want to know where to look first. Moving a program from volume to concentration is the shift risk-based regulation depends on, and it's the approach we bring to any compliance or audit brief.

What they can do now

Direct limited inspection effort to where non-compliance actually concentrates, instead of reporting how much exists city-wide. Drill from the whole CBD to a single laneway or hub. Monthly program reporting runs itself, returning more than twenty hours a month that used to go into manual assembly.

Power BI DAX Star schema SharePoint Automated refresh Governed datasets

Public data and community outcomes: from crisis response to what happened next

United States · September to October 2025 · Strategy-facing
Public data · Analysis

What it replaced

Three public datasets in three shapes, with no way to follow a crisis response through to its outcome and no shared picture anyone could plan from.

How

Started with process mapping in Miro to agree the questions the report had to answer and how a response flows through the system. Planned the integration around those questions, then used Python to pull, clean and join the 911 dispatch, health service and community resource datasets into one star-schema model built for relationship analysis. Built an executive view that answers each agreed question directly, so the report reads as a set of decisions rather than a set of charts.

What they can do now

See whether mental-health crisis interventions changed outcomes, not just how many happened. Compare response times and results by district. Put an evidence-based community-benefit picture in front of the people deciding where the next dollar of intervention funding goes: which programs are working, where the gaps are, and where to invest next.

How we work: engineering discipline for BI

This is the part a client's IT team reads to decide whether we're safe to let into their tenant.

Version control and CI/CD

Reports live in the pbip project format under Git, so every change is tracked, reviewable and reversible. Pipelines and reports deploy through Azure DevOps or GitHub across dev, test and production.

Lineage and governance

Tested before it's near your data

New platform features are built and tested on our own accounts first. An end-to-end API pipeline in Fabric runs unattended daily with error handling. We publish what we find.
