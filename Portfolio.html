01 — Enterprise Data Platforms

Microsoft Fabric architecture

Full-stack builds: ingestion, Medallion layers, dimensional modelling, governance and publication. Where the whole chain needs one owner.

Microsoft FabricKimball modelSole consultant

Enterprise hotel portfolio analytics platform

Multi-property hotel portfolio · Feb 2026 – present · End-to-end ownership, phased Agile delivery with sign-off at each milestone

The chaos

Source workbooks that could not feed Power BI at all. Benchmarking reports spread across 17–23 sheets with merged headers. Debt schedules over 11 tabs. Investment summaries with duplicate and missing codes. Budgets across 31 tabs.

The clarity

One governed semantic model where financial, benchmarking, debt and budget data filter together, published as a Power BI App across dev, test and production — with tie-out reports proving the numbers against the accounting system.

What I built

SharePoint libraries connected to Fabric via Microsoft Graph API, landing files into Bronze automatically. PySpark notebooks in Silver to parse and normalise each source — P&L anchored to the chart of accounts, merged-header benchmarking reports flattened, debt schemas normalised, budgets restructured into flat facts. Gold built as a Kimball dimensional model with conformed property, date, account, department and competitive-set dimensions, feeding a central enterprise semantic model with over 600 DAX measures. Room-weighted aggregation for portfolio-level indices. Role-specific dashboards: executive, operations, benchmarking, owner and investor, and debt and investment. Currently in delivery: franchise and brand compliance, then row-level security, governance and training.

02 — Government & Compliance

Reporting as evidence

Programs where the reporting isn't management information — it's the record of whether obligations were met.

Compliance reportingLocal governmentOngoing

City of Melbourne laneway waste audit program

Engaged by Waste Audit & Consultancy Services on contract · Jun 2025 – present · Ongoing update support and dashboard maintenance

The chaos

Audit data on bin compliance, compactor operations, dumped rubbish and infrastructure condition split between internal audit records and third-party contractor feeds, arriving on different daily, weekly and monthly cycles.

The clarity

One governed star-schema model with automated refresh — and a shift in the question the program could answer: not how much non-compliance existed, but where it concentrated.

What I built

Internal audit and contractor sources integrated into a single star-schema model handling three refresh cadences via SharePoint with automated refresh. More than 25 custom visuals and DAX measures covering compliance trends, damage-type analysis and operational status, with drill-through from a CBD-wide view down to individual laneways and hubs. Metadata documentation and governed datasets published in Power BI Service, with continuing update support after delivery.

Power BIDAXStar-schema modellingSharePointAutomated refreshGoverned datasetsMetadata documentation

Why this one matters

Regulators and public programs don't want more numbers — they want to know where to direct limited attention first. Moving a program from volume to concentration is the same shift risk-based regulation depends on, and it's the thinking I bring to any compliance or audit reporting brief.
