The problem we solve

When your numbers don't agree, the dashboard isn't the problem.

Your data lives in systems that were never designed to talk to each other. A dashboard on top just shows the disagreement faster. The real problem sits one layer down — the systems are siloed, and the same measure is defined differently in each one. You might recognise some of these.

Every system tells a different storySales, finance and ops each pull from their own tool and quote different numbers — and each swears theirs is right.
Month-end is manualExporting, combining and reconciling spreadsheets for days before anyone trusts a single figure.
No single source of truthNobody has one reliable view of how the business is actually performing right now.
You've outgrown ExcelThe business got more complex, but the reporting is still held together by workbooks and copy-paste.
Reporting you can't produce on demandA board, lender or regulator asks for numbers you simply can't pull together quickly.
It all lives in one person's headEverything stalls when the one person who knows how the systems connect is away.

We don't just build dashboards. We build the integrated layer underneath — the part that brings your systems together and makes the numbers agree.

Your systems were bought at different times, for different jobs. None of them was meant to give you a view of the whole business. So the data sits in silos, and the same word means a different number depending on which tool you ask.

We connect those systems, bring the data into one place, and define each measure once — so revenue means revenue everywhere, and every team reads from the same number. Then the dashboard on top is finally worth having, and your reporting lives in a governed platform your own team can run — not on someone's laptop.
