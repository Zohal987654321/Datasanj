The problem we solve

Why multi-site reporting breaks

We don't just build dashboards. We build the layer underneath that makes your sites comparable in the first place.

A dashboard drawn on top of inconsistent data just displays the inconsistency faster. With one site, reporting is arithmetic. With fifteen, it's a definitions problem — every site keeps its own workbook and defines the measures slightly differently, so the same word means a different number depending on who you ask.

We bring the data together and define each measure once, for every site. Then the numbers line up, comparisons are real, and the dashboard on top is worth having.

Roll-ups that don't reconcile The portfolio total doesn't equal the sum of the sites, and nobody can say which one is wrong.
Unfair comparisons Sites of different sizes ranked on raw totals, so the biggest always looks best and the ranking tells you nothing.
Slow site onboarding A new location takes weeks to appear in reporting because someone has to hand-wire it in.
Fifteen versions of one file Access managed by emailing different workbooks to different managers, with no way to know who has what.
