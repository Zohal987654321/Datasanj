Questions about Microsoft Fabric

What is Microsoft Fabric, in plain words?

One Microsoft platform where all your business data lives, gets cleaned and is turned into reports and AI answers. Microsoft calls its storage layer, OneLake, the OneDrive for data: one place, one copy, shared safely across the business. Power BI is part of Fabric.

Does a small business need Fabric, or is Power BI enough?

Often Power BI alone is enough. Fabric pays off when you have three or more systems to combine, data that is too large or slow for Power BI on its own, a need for history and audit trails, or plans to use Copilot on your reports. We tell you which on the free call.

Do we have to move all our data into Fabric?

No. Shortcuts let Fabric read data where it already sits, in Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks or on-premises, without copying it. Mirroring keeps a live copy of a database in sync with no pipelines to build.

Will our data be ready for Copilot and AI later?

Yes. The model your reports use gets agreed definitions, plain-word descriptions, tested security and data health monitoring. We can set up Copilot on your Power BI reports, and it answers from those checked numbers with the same access rules as your reports.

How much does Fabric cost to start?

Fabric is billed by capacity, and the right size depends on your data and users. Smaller capacities can be paused when not in use, and many businesses start with a pilot on one or two sources. We estimate the running cost on the free call, and our own work starts from AUD 90 an hour, with a written estimate agreed before we start.

We already use Power BI. What changes?

Your reports keep working. Fabric adds a governed place underneath them to bring data together, clean it and keep history, so the same reports become faster, easier to trust and ready for Copilot.

Which systems can you connect to Fabric?

Xero, QuickBooks, Dynamics 365, CRMs, SQL databases, SharePoint, Excel, Google Sheets, BigQuery, Shopify and most systems that can export or share data.
