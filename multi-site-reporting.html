The hidden problem

Every branch you acquired writes names differently

Each branch kept its own system and its own habits. So one customer can appear three times, and nobody can tell if they are the same person. Totals double-count and the group view is wrong.

Branch AJonathan Smith · 12 King St · 0412 555 018
Branch BJ. Smith · 12 King Street · jon.smith@mail.com
Branch CSMITH, JON · 0412555018
same person
Jonathan SmithOne customer · 3 records linked

This is a hard problem. We solve it with advanced data science techniques, known as entity resolution, so you get centralised visibility of every customer across every branch.

How we find out if they are the same person

  1. CleanTidy names, addresses and phone numbers into one format: "St" and "Street", spaces and capitals.
  2. CompareCheck name, address, phone, email and date of birth together, allowing for nicknames and typos.
  3. ScoreGive each pair a match score. Strong matches are linked automatically.
  4. ConfirmUnsure matches go to a person to check. Nothing is guessed.

The same method works for suppliers, products and the branches themselves, and it keeps working as new records arrive.

Questions group owners ask

Our sites use different systems and spreadsheets. Can you still combine them?

Yes. Each site's files or system exports are mapped to the same accounts and rules as they come in, so the group view is built automatically, however each site sends its numbers.

How do you compare sites of different sizes fairly?

By comparing per unit as well as in total: per room, per square metre, per staff hour or per customer, whichever fits your business. A small site doing well is no longer hidden behind a big site's totals.

How do you know if two customers from different branches are the same person?

This is a hard problem, solved with advanced data science techniques known as entity resolution. We compare names, addresses, phones and emails, allowing for typos and nicknames, link strong matches automatically and send unsure ones to a person to confirm. The result is centralised visibility of every customer across every branch.

Can each manager see only their own site?

Yes. Row-level security means each manager sees their site, area managers see their area, and owners see the group, all from the same report. It is set up and tested before go-live.

Will the group total match our accounts?

Yes. The group total equals the sum of the sites, and it is reconciled to your ledger before handover.

Can we add new sites later?

Yes. A new site is added by mapping its data once. The reports, comparisons and security pick it up automatically.

How much does it cost?

From AUD 90 an hour. Before any work starts you get a written estimate of the hours for each stage. The hours depend on how many sites and entities you have, how different their data is, and how many audiences need their own view.

Do we need Microsoft Fabric?

Not always. A few sites with clean data can run on Power BI alone. Many sites, many files, or lender and investor reporting usually need Fabric underneath. We tell you which on the free call.
