User in two RLS roles? How Power BI combines them

By Zohal Zahir · November 2026 · From the series Who sees what: RLS in Power BI

ShareLinkedInFacebookXEmail

The problem. Someone is in two RLS roles. You expect them to see less. They see more.

Why. Power BI adds roles together. A row shows if any of the person's roles allows it.

Example:

  • Role 1: [Product] = "Bikes"
  • Role 2: [Region] = "East"

Someone in both sees all of East, plus Bikes in every region.

The rules:

  1. One role, two filters → both must match (AND)
  2. Two roles → either one is enough (OR)
  3. A "no" in one role can't cancel a "yes" in another. A role with FALSE() on a table doesn't stop another role from showing it.
  4. A role with no rules shows every row.

The fix. Want East Bikes only? Put both filters in one role. Give each person one role that covers everything they need. People often get roles through security groups, so check group membership too.

Test the real combination: View as → tick every role the person holds.

Check before go-live

  • Each audience has one role with all its filters Desktop
  • Group memberships were checked for people in more than one role Service
  • View as was run with all of a person's roles ticked together
