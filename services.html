Services

Microsoft Fabric & Power BI consultancy services.

End-to-end delivery for growing organisations across Australia — from connecting the systems you already run, through to the reporting your team opens each morning, all agreeing on one set of numbers. Microsoft certified, Canberra-based, principal-led.

What we offer

Nine services, one connected platform.

01

Data Strategy & Roadmap

Work out what your reporting needs to answer, which systems to bring together, which platform fits, and in what order to build it — before anyone commits budget.

02

Microsoft Fabric Implementation

Full platform build on Microsoft Fabric — lakehouse, medallion architecture, notebooks and deployment pipelines, set up properly from the start.

03

Data Integration & Pipelines

Connect the systems that were never built to talk to each other — Xero, your CRM, POS, payroll, databases and file feeds — into one place, refreshing automatically instead of by hand.

04

Data Modelling & Semantic Layer

Dimensional models and DAX built to best practice, so each measure is defined once and means the same thing everywhere, and reports stay fast as your data grows.

05

Dashboard & Report Development

Reports built for the decisions people actually make — executive, operational and finance views, all on one shared model so they agree with each other.

06

Multi-Site & Multi-Entity Reporting

Consolidate branches, entities or properties so their numbers are genuinely comparable and the group roll-up reconciles to the sum of its parts.

07

Data Governance & Security

Row-level security, data quality monitoring, lineage from source system to published number, and a record of how personal information is handled. See how it's built →

08

Financial Reporting Automation

Live reporting from Xero or QuickBooks Online — AP and AR ageing, cash position, budget and forecast, without monthly exports.

09

Training & Handover

Your team trained to run and extend what we build, with the model documented and everything under version control.
