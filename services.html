Services and packages

Every engagement starts with a 30-minute fit call. A Discovery scopes the work and you receive a written proposal with a fixed price before anything is built. Delivery is lean and phased: something usable early, sign-off before the next phase.

Three service lines

Data platforms and integration

Ingestion from every source into a Fabric lakehouse. Medallion layers, Kimball dimensional modelling, one governed semantic model, row-level security, dev/test/prod deployment. APIs, SharePoint, databases, files. Documented and traceable end to end.

Analysis and modelling

Multi-site and multi-entity comparison that adjusts for size. Month-end and board reporting automation. Margin, utilisation and outcome analysis. Budget, forecast and scenario. Public-data and program evaluation work.

Reporting and visualisation

Executive, operational, finance and compliance reporting on one model. Board and investor packs. Custom visuals where a standard chart doesn't answer the question.

Questions your team can finally answer

Once your systems feed one model, questions that used to take a week of spreadsheet work are answered by opening a report. A few we build for most often.

Finance

  • Are we cash positive this month, and how much are we owed?
  • Which customers are overdue, and by how much?
  • How are we tracking against budget, by entity and by cost centre?
  • What does the consolidated P&L look like across all entities, today?

Operations

  • Which site or property is underperforming once you adjust for size?
  • Where does margin actually come from: which products, jobs or services?
  • Which supplier is late, and what did it cost us?
  • What is utilisation this week, by team and by role?

Board and investors

  • What is profit per property, per branch, per entity?
  • What do lenders and investors need to see, and can we produce it on demand?
  • Are we on plan, and where is the variance coming from?

Compliance and outcomes

  • Where does non-compliance concentrate, so inspection effort goes there first?
  • Did the program change outcomes, or just count activity?
  • Can we trace this number on the report back to the source record it came from?

Every number, defined once

The reason two reports disagree is rarely a bug. It's that "revenue" or "active customer" or "on time" means something slightly different in each system. We agree one definition for every measure, write it down, and build it into the model, so finance, operations and the board are reading the same number, and anyone can see how it was calculated.
