AI-ready Power BI and Fabric

AI is only as reliable as the foundation under it. We build the foundation.

Copilot, ChatGPT and AI agents can only answer from the data and the context they are given. If "revenue" lives in five spreadsheets with five meanings, AI gives five answers, confidently. A strong, correct foundation is centralised, governed data in Microsoft Fabric and Power BI models built to be read by AI. That is the work we do.

  • Centralised in one place. Your systems feed one governed Fabric platform and one semantic model, not scattered files and copies.
  • One definition per number. Each measure is defined once and described in plain words, so AI knows what "revenue" or "active customer" means for your business.
  • Governed, clean data. Data-quality checks, owners for each dataset, Purview lineage and version control, so you know where every number came from and who changed what.
  • Security that AI respects. Row-level security means people only get AI answers from data they are allowed to see.
  • Data health monitoring. Checks on every refresh for late, missing or unusual data, with an alert before a wrong number reaches a report or an AI answer.
  • Traceable answers. Every number can be followed back to its source, so you can check what AI tells you.
  • Copilot on your reports. We set up Copilot in Power BI on your reports, so people can ask questions in plain English and get answers from the same checked numbers.

Strong foundations first. Reliable AI follows.

Questions businesses ask us

Can we use Copilot or ChatGPT on our data?

Yes, once the data underneath is ready. AI answers from the definitions and data it is given, so we build one trusted model with plain-word descriptions and security first. Then we can set up Copilot on your Power BI reports, so it answers from the same checked numbers. Copilot needs a paid Fabric capacity; we size it on the free call.

How do I automate month-end reporting?

Connect your accounting system (like Xero or QuickBooks) to Power BI so the profit and loss, balance sheet and cash reports refresh on their own. No more exporting to Excel each month.

How do I consolidate reports from multiple companies or sites?

Bring every entity into one data model with the same account mapping, then report each one side by side and as a group total that adds up.

How do I make a cash flow forecast I can trust?

Use your real actuals for past months and your budget for future months, with a few simple assumptions you control. It updates when your accounts do.

Why is my Power BI report so slow?

Usually the data model, heavy measures or the refresh setup. We find the cause, fix it and write down what changed.

How do I stop managers seeing other teams' data in Power BI?

Row-level security filters each report so people only see their own rows. We set it up or check it, and test it. Our free guide shows how.

How much does it cost?

AUD 90 an hour. Before any work starts you get a written estimate of the hours for each stage. If something needs more time, we tell you and you approve it first. Small fixes take a few hours. Automating monthly statements and a forecast from your accounting exports is typically around AUD 4,000. Bigger projects are built in stages you sign off.

Do we need Microsoft Fabric?

Not always. Many jobs only need Power BI and the systems you already have. When Fabric makes sense.

Which systems can you connect?

Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, CRMs, databases, SharePoint, Excel, Google Sheets and most systems that can export or share data.

Yes. Onsite in the ACT, and remotely across Australia, New Zealand and the United States.
