What we offer

01

Data Strategy & Roadmap

Work out what your reporting actually needs to answer, which platform fits, and in what order to build it — before anyone commits budget.

Microsoft Fabric Implementation

Full platform build on Microsoft Fabric — lakehouse, medallion architecture, notebooks and deployment pipelines, set up properly from the start.

03

Data Integration & Pipelines

Connect your accounting system, operational software, databases and file feeds into one place, refreshing automatically instead of by hand.

Dashboard & Report Development

Multi-Site Reporting

Consolidate branches, entities or properties so their numbers are genuinely comparable and roll-ups reconcile to the sum of their parts.

Data Governance & Security

Financial Reporting Automation
