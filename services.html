Where most engagements start

Reporting diagnostic

Before recommending a build, I trace the chain from source system to decision and find where it breaks. Most organisations don't need what they think they need, and finding that out early is considerably cheaper than finding it out during implementation.

A diagnostic is a fixed-scope piece of work that gives you a clear picture whether or not you go on to engage me for the build:

  • Where definitions are contested, undocumented, or implicit in someone's spreadsheet
  • Where the grain of your data doesn't support the questions being asked of it
  • Which manual steps carry real risk, and which are merely annoying
  • Structural and scalability limits in the existing model
  • A prioritised recommendation, including anything you should not spend money on

What a diagnostic gives you

Sometimes the honest answer is that you have a process problem, an ownership problem, or a data-entry problem, and no amount of Power BI will fix it. I would rather tell you that in week one than build something that quietly inherits it.

Engagement model

How I work

Phased, with sign-off at each stage. You should never be several months into a build before you see something you can use.

1

Assess

We trace the chain from source system to decision and find where it breaks — undefined grain, contested definitions, manual steps, unowned handoffs. Scope, risks and outcomes get defined before anything is built.

2

Build in phases

Pipeline, then model, then reporting, delivered in Agile milestones with sign-off at each one. Figures are reconciled to source as we go, so trust is established during the build rather than argued about after it.

3

Govern and hand over

Row-level security, deployment pipelines, version control and documentation, plus training. The measure of a good handover is that your team can change the model without me.

4

Support, if you want it

Monthly retainer for maintenance, new development and priority response — used by clients whose reporting keeps evolving. Optional, and never a condition of the build.

Fit and investment

Who this suits

I work on engagements that need proper scoping and accountability for outcomes. That tends to mean organisations where:

  • Reporting already drives decisions rather than just describing them
  • Accuracy and defensibility are non-negotiable — regulated, audited, or board-reported
  • The cost of getting it wrong exceeds the cost of doing it properly
  • There's appetite for structured discovery rather than an immediate build

On fees

Fees are set after discovery, once complexity and responsibility are understood. Quoting before that either pads the estimate or produces a scope change later, and neither serves you. Engagements range from a focused diagnostic review through to a complete platform implementation, with ongoing support available by monthly retainer.

If you're after a quick, low-cost dashboard, I'm not the right fit — and I'd rather say so here than in a first call.

Confidentiality

How I handle your data

You won't find client dashboards or client figures anywhere on this site. That's deliberate, and it's the same standard your work gets.

01

Nothing published, ever

No client data, screenshots or figures appear on this site, on LinkedIn, or in a proposal to anyone else. Where I describe past work, it is described in general terms with the client's agreement.

02

Your environment, not mine

I work inside your tenant wherever possible rather than taking copies of your data. Nothing lands on personal devices or third-party tools without your written agreement.

03

Access controls from day one

Row-level security is designed in from the start, not added later, so each site or department sees only what it should. Access I hold is the minimum the work requires, and it ends when the work does.

04

Agreements before access

I sign a confidentiality agreement before touching data, and I'm glad to work under yours. Australian citizen, Canberra based, and able to obtain a baseline security clearance if your work requires one.

On demonstrations

Any dashboard I show you in a call or publish as an example is built on data I generate for the purpose — never a client's. If you'd like to see how something works before engaging me, I'd rather build you a small demonstration than show you someone else's numbers.
